JJ's IT Services — Archived Incident Report
Service Type: Network / Residential Diagnostics
Location: Private Residence
Status: Closed
Reference: SR-0417

The original call sounded routine. The homeowner reported intermittent Wi-Fi failures, several cameras going offline, and an unexplained Outage affecting devices on the second floor.

The house was old, but the network equipment was relatively new. Initial testing showed no obvious problem with the router, modem, or cabling. Strangely, one camera continued reporting a healthy Gateway connection even after its Ethernet cable had been physically disconnected.

A second inspection found an unused cable disappearing behind the wall. The homeowner said nobody knew where it went. Following it through the crawlspace revealed an old network switch sitting on a wooden beam. There was no visible power connection, yet its activitY lights were blinking.

At approximately 11:43 PM, the laptop being used for diagnostics briefly displayed a new device. Its Hostname consisted only of numbers and disappeared before a connection could be made.

The switch was removed and all known network equipment was Checked. Nothing unusual was found inside the device, and no battery or secondary power source was present.

Before leaving, the camera system was tested one final time. Every camera appeared normal except one covering the upstairs hallway. Reviewing the footage showed a brief Loss of video at exactly 11:43 PM.

Three frames later, the feed returned. For less than a second, an unidentified device appeared in the network inventory under an Old address that had not been assigned to anything in years.

No further faults could be reproduced. The homeowner was advised to monitor the system and report any New activity.

The service request was eventually closed as unresolved. The removed switch was retained for inspection, although subsequent Testing produced no unusual behavior.

One detail remains unexplained: the diagnostic log contains a final Entry timestamped several minutes after the laptop had already been shut down.
